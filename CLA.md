By contributing to this repository you agree to the terms below. A bot will ask
you to confirm on your first pull request.

## Why this exists, plainly

The project's licence may need to change as the platform is established -- ADR-011
forecasts where and why. **Without this agreement every contributor holds a veto
on that**, because their copyright stays theirs and their code stays under the
licence it arrived with. Projects that took contributions under a sign-off alone
and later needed to move had to rewrite or remove contributed code.

This agreement does not loosen the licence and does not take your copyright. It
grants the project the rights needed to keep choosing.
